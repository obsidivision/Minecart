Floatcart simulator rules

Every Minecraft rule used to simulate FarCoolHat's vertical floatcart challenge tick by tick, in Java Edition 26.2. See the build and both runs in 3D.

  • 67 rules

A floatcart is a minecart whose top sits less than 1e-5 of a block above a block boundary (rule X3). The simulator applies the rules below in the game's order, and together they reproduce the creator's stored attempt bit for bit (y = 1.2967236180145676).

Each rule was checked against the game's decompiled source. In an ID, the letter is the topic and the number follows the order in the code. Full rule shows the exact wording from the simulator's code and the file it is in.

Minecart basics 8

What a minecart is and the order of each game tick.

M1

Size

The hitbox is 0.98 wide and 0.7 tall; the cart's position is the bottom centre.

A minecart's hitbox is 0.98 wide and 0.7 tall (as floats: half-width 0.49000000953674316, height 0.699999988079071); its position is the bottom centre.

Full rule physics.py

This world runs the classic minecart physics (the experimental minecart-improvements option is off); both stored carts sit exactly where the classic track-height rule (R2) puts them.

M3

Placing

A minecart item on a rail spawns the cart at the rail block + (0.5, 1/16, 0.5), or half a block higher on a slope.

Full rule physics.py

A minecart item used on a rail spawns the cart at the rail block + (0.5, 1/16, 0.5), half a block higher on a slope.

M4

Tick order

Gravity, then the rail or off-rail move, then block effects, then the water check (used from the next tick).

Full rule physics.py

Gravity, then the rail or off-rail move, then block effects, then the water check (which sets "in water" for the next tick).

M5

Gravity

Vertical speed drops by 0.04 per tick, or 0.005 if the cart was in water.

Full rule physics.py

Every tick a cart's vertical speed drops by 0.04, or by 0.005 if it was in water.

M6

Rail lookup

If the block below the cart's feet is a rail, it rides that; otherwise it rides the block at its feet. So a cart sitting on honey rides the rail under the honey.

Full rule physics.py

The cart rides the block under its feet block if that is a rail, otherwise the block at its feet. So a cart resting on honey (feet at y + 15/16, inside the honey's cell) rides the rail under the honey block.

M7

Block effects after every move

Block effects (honey, detector rails) run after every move, not once per tick.

Full rule physics.py

In the classic engine a minecart's inside-block effects (honey slide, detector rails) run after every move, not once per tick.

M8

Fall distance

Grows while falling and resets on the ground, on rails, in water and on a honey slide. It's tracked but never changes the motion.

Full rule physics.py

It grows while the cart falls outside water and resets on the ground, on a rail, in water and on a honey slide. It is tracked here but never changes the motion.

R

Riding the rails 11

How a cart moves along a rail in the classic engine, step by step.

R1

Track ends

Every rail shape has two ends; the low end of a slope is one block down.

Full rule oldcart.py

Every rail shape has two ends, given as offsets from the rail block; the low end of a slope is one block down (ascending_north: high end north, low end south).

R2

Height on the track

1/16 above the rail block on flat track. On a slope it rises evenly from 1/16 at the low edge to 1 1/16 at the high edge.

Full rule oldcart.py

On a flat rail the cart rides 1/16 above the rail block; on a slope its height rises evenly from 1/16 at the low edge to 1 1/16 at the high edge. (So on a slope the height above the rail block is 1/16 plus the distance from the low edge.)

R3

Slope pull

+1/128 block/tick toward the low end (×0.2 in water).

Full rule physics.py

On a slope the cart gains 1/128 block/tick of speed toward the low end (x0.2 in water).

R4

Steering

Velocity is turned along the track toward the end the cart is heading for; speed is capped at 2.

Full rule physics.py

The horizontal velocity is turned to point along the track, toward the end the cart is already heading for, and its speed is capped at 2.

R5

Centring

Before moving, the cart is put on the track's centre line at the rail block's height, one block higher on a slope. That puts a cart under the honey inside the honey block, which is why it moves at ×0.4.

Full rule physics.py

Before moving, the cart is put on the track's centre line at the rail block's height, one block higher on a slope. A cart creeping under a honey block is therefore inside the honey while it moves (H1: speed x0.4).

R6

Move cap

At most 0.4 per axis per tick (0.2 in water); with a passenger it moves 75% of its velocity.

Full rule physics.py

The cart moves by its velocity, capped at 0.4 per axis (0.2 in water); with a passenger it moves 75% of its velocity.

R7

Leaving a slope

Moving past a slope's low end drops the cart one block.

Full rule physics.py

If the move takes the cart into the cell beyond the slope's low end, it drops one block (so the track height is looked up one level down).

R8

Rail friction

The cart keeps 96% of its speed each tick (99.7% with a passenger), and vertical speed is zeroed.

Full rule physics.py

The cart keeps 96% of its speed each tick (99.7% with a passenger) and loses its vertical speed.

R9

Water drag

In water it then keeps only 95% of that.

Full rule physics.py

A cart in water then keeps only 95% of that.

R10

Height and speed

Speed changes by 0.05 × the height lost this tick; then y is set to the track height.

Full rule physics.py

Speed changes by 0.05 x (height lost this tick): faster going down, slower going up. Then y is set to the track height (R2).

R11

New block

On entering a new block, velocity is re-aimed along that step at the same speed.

Full rule physics.py

When the cart crosses into another block, its velocity is re-aimed straight along that step, keeping its speed.

Powered rails and redstone 6

Boosting, braking and how the levers power the rails.

P1

Boost

On a powered rail that's ON, a cart faster than 0.01 gains 0.06 block/tick in its direction of travel.

Full rule physics.py

On a powered rail that is ON, a cart faster than 0.01 gains 0.06 block/tick in its direction of travel, every tick.

P2

Standing start

On a flat powered rail that's ON, a slower cart is pushed at 0.02 away from a conductor block at one end. West or north is checked first.

Full rule physics.py

On a flat powered rail that is ON, a slower cart is pushed at 0.02 away from a conductor block (P4) at one end; west or north is checked first.

P3

Halt rail

A powered rail that's OFF stops a cart slower than 0.03 dead; otherwise it halves its speed.

Full rule physics.py

A powered rail that is OFF brakes the cart: below speed 0.03 it stops dead, otherwise its speed is halved (and vertical speed zeroed).

P4

Conductors

Concrete and note blocks conduct redstone. Glass, grates, observers, pistons, honey, buds, rails, levers and signs don't.

Full rule physics.py

Concrete and note blocks conduct redstone; glass, copper grates, observers, pistons, honey, amethyst buds, rails, levers and signs don't.

P5

Lever

A lever powers the block it's attached to. The lower lever's concrete powers rail (2,2,6).

Full rule verify_lever_solution.py

A lever powers the block it is attached to. The lower lever (1,2,5) sits on the concrete at (2,2,5), and that powered block powers rail (2,2,6) next to it.

P6

Rail power chain

A powered rail is also ON if it connects along the track through powered rails to one that has power, within 8 rails. So (2,1,7) switches together with (2,2,6).

Full rule verify_lever_solution.py

A powered rail is also ON when it connects along the track, through ON powered rails, to one that has power from a block, within 8 rails. (2,1,7) gets its power from (2,2,6) this way, so the lever switches both together.

Off the rails, horizontal speed is capped at 0.4 per axis (0.2 in water).

Collisions and block shapes 13

Hitboxes, block shapes and how collisions are resolved.

C1

Axis order

A move is resolved vertical axis first, then the horizontal axis with the bigger move.

Full rule physics.py

A move is resolved one axis at a time: vertical first, then the horizontal axis with the bigger move (x first on ties). (B1).

Relies on B1.

C2

Collision check

The blocks checked are the ones whose boxes overlap the hitbox stretched along the move.

Full rule physics.py

The blocks tested for a move are those whose collision boxes overlap the hitbox stretched along the move.

Relies on B2.

C3

Collision tolerance

The tolerance is 1e-7. A box the mover already overlaps doesn't block it, and moves under 1e-7 count as zero.

Full rule physics.py

A block face stops a mover only if the mover overlaps it sideways by more than 1e-7 and is no more than 1e-7 past the face already; a box the mover already overlaps does not block it; a move shorter than 1e-7 on an axis counts as zero.

C4

Tiny moves

If collisions shrink a move to almost nothing (squared length 1e-7 or less), the cart doesn't move at all.

Full rule physics.py

If collisions cut a move down to a squared length of 1e-7 or less (and by at least 1e-7), the cart does not move at all.

C5

No bounce

A blocked direction loses all its speed.

Full rule physics.py

A blocked axis loses all its speed (minecarts don't bounce, and the blocks here add no bounce, B3). A horizontal axis counts as blocked when the allowed move differs from the wanted one by 1e-5 or more.

Relies on B3.

C6

On the ground

The cart is on the ground when a downward move is blocked. Its supporting block is the nearest one in a thin slab under the hitbox.

Full rule physics.py

The cart is on the ground when a downward move was blocked. Its supporting block is the nearest block (centre to cart position) inside a 1e-6 thin slab under its hitbox; if there is none, the slab is tried again shifted back along the move's horizontal part.

C7

Block under the feet

Found from the supporting block's column; walls, fence gates and fences are special cases.

Full rule physics.py

With a supporting block (C6), it is the block in that block's column at floor(y - offset); a wall or fence-gate support (or a fence, for offsets up to 0.5) counts as itself, and so does any support when the offset is 1e-5 or less. Without one, it is the block at floor(position - offset).

C8

Full cubes

Glass, concrete, observers, note blocks, retracted pistons, and the copper grate.

Full rule physics.py

Glass, concrete, observers, note blocks, retracted pistons and the copper grate (B4) collide as whole 1x1x1 blocks.

Relies on B4.

C9

No collision

Rails, levers, signs and air have no collision box.

Full rule physics.py

Rails, levers, signs and air have no collision box.

C10

Honey block

Its box is 14/16 wide and 15/16 tall, so a cart lands on it at y + 0.9375.

Full rule physics.py

Its box is 14/16 wide (1/16 in from every side) and 15/16 tall, so a cart landing on it rests at y + 0.9375.

C11

Amethyst buds

They stick out 3/4/5/7 sixteenths (small/medium/large/cluster). The medium bud's face here is at z = 6.25.

Full rule physics.py

A box sticking out of the face the bud grows on, 3/16 (small), 4/16 (medium), 5/16 (large) or 7/16 (cluster) long and 8/16 (small) or 10/16 wide, centred on that face. A medium bud pointing south fills z 0..4/16 of its block, so its face is at z = 6.25 in this build.

C12

Strict overlap

Boxes that only touch don't count as overlapping.

Full rule physics.py

Two boxes that only touch do not overlap.

C13

Solid faces

Only full cubes have solid side faces here.

Full rule physics.py

In this build only full cubes have solid side faces; air, rails, signs, levers, honey and buds don't.

H

Honey 2

The two honey mechanics the cart runs into.

H1

Speed factor

After every move, horizontal speed is multiplied by the speed factor of the block the cart is in, or else the block 0.500001 below its feet. Honey is 0.4, everything else 1, and it's always 1 while the cart is in a rail block.

Full rule physics.py

After every move, horizontal speed is multiplied by the speed factor of the block the cart is in; if that is 1, the block 0.500001 below its feet (C7) counts instead. Honey is 0.4, every other block here is 1. A cart whose position is in a rail block always gets 1.

H2

Honey slide

An entity falling against a honey block's side has its fall slowed to about 0.13/tick. The formula uses the gravity and drag of mobs and players, even for minecarts.

Full rule physics.py

An entity against a honey block's side (its centre more than 0.4375 + half its width from the block's centre on x or z), below the block's top and not on the ground, whose vertical speed converted as vy / 0.98 + 0.08 is below -0.08, gets vertical speed (-0.05 - 0.08) * 0.98; if the converted speed was below -0.13, its horizontal speed is also scaled by -0.05 / (that speed). The conversion uses living-entity gravity and drag even for minecarts.

Relies on B7.

W

Water 7

Waterlogged blocks, water flow and the current.

W1

Water sources

Waterlogged blocks hold a water source. The copper grate's is a falling source; the rails' and buds' are ordinary.

Full rule physics.py

Waterlogged blocks hold a water source. The copper grate's water is a falling source; waterlogged rails and buds hold ordinary sources.

W2

Water surface

A source is 8/9 of a block tall, or a full block if there's water above it.

Full rule physics.py

A source is 8/9 of a block tall, or a full block when the block above also holds water.

W3

In water

The cart counts as in water when its hitbox (shrunk by 0.001) reaches below a water surface.

Full rule physics.py

The cart is in water when its hitbox, shrunk by 0.001, reaches below the water surface of any cell it touches. The result counts from the next tick on (M5, R3, R6, R9, F1).

W4

Flow

Water flows from higher water toward lower water next to it; a dry side with water below it counts as a drop.

Full rule physics.py

Each side with water adds (its direction) x (own height - neighbour height); a dry side with water below it counts as a drop and adds (its direction) x (own height - (height below - 8/9)). Other sides add nothing.

Relies on B5.

W5

Falling water

Falling water next to a solid face gets an extra downward push. The grate's current is (0, −0.98639, 0.16440).

Full rule physics.py

If the water is falling and a side neighbour, or the block above that neighbour, has a solid face (C13), the flow is normalised, gets an extra (0, -6, 0) and is normalised again: a strong downward push. For the grate this gives (0, -0.98639, 0.16440).

Relies on B6.

W6

Current

The current pushes the cart 0.014 block/tick in the direction of the combined flow of the water it touches.

Full rule physics.py

The flows of those water cells are added up (each scaled by the depth so far while that is under 0.4), and the cart is pushed 0.014 block/tick along the total's direction; a total with squared length under 1e-5 pushes nothing. If both horizontal speeds are under 0.003 and the push is weaker than 0.0045, it is raised to 0.0045 (a minecart's push is always 0.014, so never here).

W7

Water never moves here

Lever flips make the water recheck, but it can't flow out of the grate, and rails only accept source water.

Full rule physics.py

A lever flip schedules water updates in the grate and in the waterlogged rail next to the switched rails, but water can't flow out of a full block (the grate), and rails accept only source water (B8), so the water stays exactly as built.

Relies on B8.

The tester: piston, detector rail, armor stand 13

The left station that checks a cart is float-aligned.

T1

Piston speed

A piston's head moves 0.5 block per tick.

Full rule piston_rig.py

A piston's head moves half a block per game tick, so it takes two ticks to extend or retract.

T2

Swept area

Entities in the space the head moves through each tick get pushed.

Full rule piston_rig.py

Each tick the moving head's plate sweeps the slab it moves through; entities in that slab are pushed.

T3

Push amount

Push distance = the overlap (at most 0.5) + 0.01.

Full rule piston_rig.py

A pushed entity moves by its overlap with the swept slab (at most 0.5) plus 0.01.

T4

Push limit

Pistons move an entity at most 0.51 per axis per tick.

Full rule piston_rig.py

Pistons move an entity at most 0.51 per axis in one game tick (a running total).

T5

Base yank

An entity still overlapping a retracting piston's base, even by 1e-5, is pulled back up to 0.51.

Full rule piston_rig.py

If an entity still overlaps a retracting piston's base block (even by just 1e-5) without sticking out of its far side, it is moved back against the head's motion by min(its reach into the base + 0.01, 0.5) + 0.01.

T6

Effects after a push

After any piston push, even one that moves nothing, the entity's block effects run. That's how the armor stand triggers the detector rail.

Full rule piston_rig.py

After a piston push, even one that moves nothing, the entity's inside-block effects run. That is how the armor stand makes the detector rail look for carts.

T7

Detector rail

It looks for minecarts only, 0.2 in from its sides and 0.2 below its top. While pressed, it rechecks every 20 ticks.

Full rule piston_rig.py

When an entity inside an unpowered detector rail triggers it, it looks for minecarts only, in the box 0.2 in from the block's sides and 0.2 below its top ((1.2-1.8, 2.0-2.8, 1.2-1.8) here); while pressed it re-checks every 20 ticks.

T8

Processing order

Entities in the same area are processed in storage order, so the cart moves before the armor stand.

Full rule piston_rig.py

Entities in the same section are handled in storage order, so the cart is pushed before the armor stand triggers the detector.

T9

Armor stand

0.5 wide × 1.975 tall. It can't be pushed, and it never touches the cart.

Full rule piston_rig.py

Its hitbox is 0.5 wide and 1.975 tall; other entities can't push it, and it pushes only rideable minecarts within squared distance 0.2, so it never touches the cart.

T10

Scaffolding

Scaffolding only blocks entities above it.

Full rule piston_rig.py

Collides only with entities above it; from the side the cart passes through it.

T11

Piston head

The plate is 4/16 thick. On the second retraction tick it sweeps x 1.75–2.25.

Full rule piston_rig.py

The plate is a full 16x16 face, 4/16 thick. On retraction tick 2 it sits half a block out, sweeping x 1.75-2.25 at y 2-3.

T12

Wall

A wall post is 8/16 wide and 1.5 blocks tall for collisions, so it stops the cart at minX 1.75.

Full rule piston_rig.py

A wall post is 8/16 wide and 1.5 blocks tall for collisions, so the post at (1,0,1) stops the pulled-back cart at minX = 1.75.

Relies on A7.

T13

Trigger

Changing the note block makes the observer pulse the piston; the exact timing doesn't matter.

Full rule piston_rig.py

Changing the note block makes the observer pulse the piston (out, then back). The exact pulse timing doesn't matter: an unfinished extension is completed before the retraction starts.

Relies on A5.

Exact numbers 4

Float rounding, epsilons and the floatcart window itself.

X1

Floats

Values the game stores as 32-bit floats (0.98, 0.7, 0.4, 0.95, 1e-5 …) are rounded to float precision.

Full rule oldcart.py

Numbers the game keeps as 32-bit floats (0.98, 0.7, 0.4, 0.95, 1e-5, ...) are rounded to float precision before they are used with doubles.

X2

Block-effect shrink

Block-effect checks use the hitbox shrunk by 1e-5.

Full rule physics.py

Inside-block checks use the hitbox shrunk by 1e-5 on every side.

X3

Floatcart window

The y-fraction must be strictly between 0.30000001192092896 and 0.30001001192067633. There, overlap tests see the cart touching the block above, but block-effect checks don't.

Full rule piston_rig.py

A cart is float-aligned when its top pokes above a block boundary by less than 1e-5: y-fraction strictly between 0.30000001192092896 and 0.30001001192067633. Exact overlap tests (C12) then see it touching the block above, but inside-block checks (X2) don't.

What's assumed

The game files for these are not in the project, so they could not be checked against source. The bit-for-bit match with the creator's stored attempt is consistent with all of them.

  • B1 Axis order: vertical first, then the bigger horizontal move. Used by C1.
  • B2 The order blocks are tested in (x fastest, then y, then z). It only changes results below 1e-7. Used by C2.
  • B3 Glass, honey, the grate and buds make nothing bounce. Used by C5.
  • B4 The copper grate collides as a full cube. Used by C8.
  • B5 Air and rails don't block water flow. Whether glass does changes nothing here. Used by W4.
  • B6 A flow vector shorter than 1e-5 counts as zero. Used by W5.
  • B7 A block's "inside" space is the whole block. Used by H2.
  • B8 Rails accept only source water, never flowing water. Used by W7.
  • A5 Observer and note block timing. The test's outcome doesn't depend on it. Used by T13.
  • A7 Wall collision: a post 8/16 wide and 1.5 blocks tall. Used by T12.
