How it works

A track is one straight lane of rails. The cart rolls from the first rail and stops on the last one, a powered rail that is off, sloping up. How far up it gets is the alignment: y = 1/16 plus that.

The game's physics
A port of the simulator that lands on exactly the number FarCoolHat's stored cart has, confirmed in game on 13 tracks. 7,980 runs against the original, every digit the same.
x and z
Break the parking rail: a parked cart has no speed, so it drops straight down and keeps both. Checked on 2,100 drops.
Starting against a stopper
Instead of being placed, a cart can stop dead against a stopper and come to rest on the start rail. Where it stops sets where it starts: side on, a bud's width counts (medium, large and cluster are equally wide); tip first, its length does; coming from the other end mirrors it. That makes 16 starts: Try every stopper searches them all, Pick one just the one you choose.
The launcher
FarCoolHat's stopper: the cart runs off a powered slope, stops dead in mid-air against the stopper and falls straight onto the start rail, so there is nothing to break. The flight is simulated tick by tick, collisions included; it has not been tried in game yet.
Boat carts
A cart with a boat can't be placed by hand: it picks the boat up on the launcher, or on a loading run whose last glass block you break to drop it. A rider moves 0.75 of its speed and keeps 99.7% of it each tick, so these are different tracks.
Where you build
It changes the last digits. Check at 60 positions reruns a track across the world, in all four directions.
Nothing may touch the cart
Over 0.1 blocks/tick it picks up whatever it can push within 0.2 blocks, and a passenger changes everything. Keep the track clear, and leave the experimental minecart changes off.
Downloads
The schematic alone, or with command blocks that place the cart (on the launcher, with its boat, for a launched start), wait, and report the result in chat with a wool marker.
