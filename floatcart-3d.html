About this view

Blocks and entities come straight from vertical_floatcart_challenge.litematic; x runs east, z runs south. Left end: the floatcart tester (note block → observer → piston, with the detector rail). Right end: the station you launch from the top rail.

Cart motion comes from a Python simulator of Minecraft 26.2's classic minecart physics; the rules page lists every rule it applies, and each phase above links to the ones it uses. The as-built run reproduces the creator's stored cart bit for bit. The outline around each cart is its 0.98×0.7 hitbox; a floatcart's top sits a hair above a block boundary.

Glowing gold rails are powered. In the lever solution, the two lower powered rails go dark on the ticks the lever is off.

Build: “vertical floatcart challenge” by FarCoolHat. Blocks and entities come from its schematic; the cart runs are simulated with the rules on the rules page.
